\mainfig{13cm}{fig1_design}{Design of the study. (a) The ten classes of one group of the 17-class set, a resonance $X$ decaying to
two quarks or gluons, as schematic jets, with the class each falls in under the four label sets; below, all 188
classes as cells, a bar being one class of a label set and the outline the group above. (b) A network pretrained on one
label set is used through a linear probe on its frozen features or fine-tuned.}
\mainfig{15cm}{fig2_accuracy}{Accuracy on the 188 JetClass-II classes (a) and on JetClass (b) for each pretraining label set and
for the random grouping into 17 classes. Open points are linear probes on the frozen features, filled points models
fine-tuned on $10^6$ jets; the dashed line and band are the network trained from scratch. Points and bars are means
and standard deviations over runs; in (b) the axis is broken between the two uses.}
\mainfig{15cm}{fig3_classes}{(a, b) Recall of each JetClass-II class under a linear probe for the 43- and 17-class models
against the 188-class model, coloured by what the label set merges the class with; open points lie within three
standard deviations of equal recall. (c) One minus the AUC of a binary linear probe for pairs of classes, for each label
set and the random groupings; open markers where the labels put the pair in one class. Means and standard deviations
over runs.}
\mainfig{15cm}{fig5_resonance}{Sophon's $X\to b\bar b$ (a) and $X\to bs$ (b) benchmark: signal efficiency against QCD
efficiency in the mass window, by transfer learning on the frozen features of each label set, for the same classifier
trained from scratch and, dash-dotted, from the output scores where the label set keeps $X\to b\bar b$. Lower panels
give the ratio to the 188-class model; the dotted line marks 1\% background efficiency. Bands are standard deviations
over runs.}
\mainfig{15cm}{fig4_training_size}{Accuracy of fine-tuned models on JetClass-II (a) and JetClass (b) against the number of
training jets, for each label set and for the network trained from scratch. Lines and bands are means and standard
deviations over runs. Lower panels give each label set's difference to the 188-class model in percentage points, with
both run spreads combined.}
\mainfig{15cm}{figA1}{Recall of each JetClass-II class under a linear probe on the frozen features of the (a) 162-, (b)

combining run spread and test-sample uncertainty; colors and shapes give what the panel's label set merges the class
with. Open points lie within three standard deviations of equal recall.}
\mainfig{15cm}{figA2}{Background rejection against signal efficiency for three pairs of resonance classes, from binary
linear probes on the frozen features, for the four label sets and the random groupings that merge the pair or keep it
apart. Each panel starts where the 188-class curve does. Lower panels give the ratio to the 188-class model; bands are
standard deviations over runs.}
\mainfig{15cm}{figA6}{As figure A2, for three pairs of QCD classes. Every random grouping merges these pairs.}

\mainfig{15cm}{figA7}{As figure 5, for top tagging (a) and quark/gluon discrimination (b): background rejection
