\mainfig{13cm}{fig1_design}{Design of the study. (a) The ten classes of one group of the 17-class set, a resonance
$X$ decaying to two quarks or gluons, drawn as schematic jets, with the class each falls in under the four label sets;
below, all 188 classes as cells, where a bar spanning several cells is one class of that label set. (b) One model is
pretrained for each label set on the same jets and is then used with frozen features or fine-tuned.}
\mainfig{15cm}{fig2_frozen}{Linear probes on the frozen features: accuracy on (a) the 188 JetClass-II classes and (b)
JetClass, and background rejection Rej$_{30\%}$ on (c) top tagging and (d) quark/gluon discrimination. Points are the
mean over pretraining runs and bars the standard deviation; the line and band repeat the 188-class model across the
rows. Panels of one metric share one relative range about the 188-class mean.}
\mainfig{15cm}{fig3_pairs}{Background rejection against signal efficiency for two pairs of JetClass-II classes, from
linear probes on the frozen features: (a) $X\to b\bar b$ against $X\to c\bar c$ and (b) $X\to bc$ against $X\to bq$ and
$X\to cs$. Curves are means over runs; the random groupings are shown separately for those that merge the pair and
those that keep it apart. Lower panels give the ratio to the 188-class model, whose band is one standard deviation.}
\mainfig{15cm}{fig4_training_size}{Fine-tuned models against the number of training jets: accuracy on (a) JetClass-II
and (b) JetClass, and background rejection Rej$_{30\%}$ on (c) top tagging and (d) quark/gluon discrimination. Lines
are the mean over runs and bands the standard deviation. The same network trained from scratch is shown in grey.}
\mainfig{15cm}{fig5_resonance}{Sophon's $X\to b\bar b$ and $X\to bs$ benchmark: signal efficiency over the square
root of the QCD efficiency, against the QCD efficiency in the mass window. Solid lines are transfer learning on the
frozen features and, in grey, the same classifier trained from scratch. Dash-dotted lines in (a) use the output
scores directly, where the label set keeps $X\to b\bar b$ as a class. Bands are the standard deviation over runs, for
the 188-class model and for training from scratch.}
