\section*{Data availability}
The label maps, the per-run values behind every table and figure, the tested differences and the learning-rate
scans are in the data release that accompanies this paper.

\section*{Use of AI tools}
An AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) was used to write analysis code, to launch and monitor the training and
evaluation jobs, and to draft text.
The authors checked the results and the text and are responsible for both.
