The random network without pretraining: three initializations. The $b$-quark boundary and the random flavor boundary lie only in the four-quark decays; every label set but the \Kfull-class one merges all QCD classes. The random network without pretraining, as initialized and with its BatchNorm statistics recomputed, has no label set, no $\rho$ and no fine-tuning. A mark after two values applies to the configuration.
