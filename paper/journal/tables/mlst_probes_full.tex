\caption{Linear probes on the frozen features of the networks pretrained with 188, 162, 43 and 17 classes, of the two networks with an added mass output, of every 17-class partition, of the random networks and of the released Sophon checkpoint, at each task's largest training set. The first column blocks give JetClass-II, on its class-balanced test set, and JetClass, the later blocks top and quark/gluon tagging, also scored on Herwig jets. The columns are accuracy, AUC, the ratio $\rho$ of $1-\mathrm{AUC}$ to the \Kfull-class run of the same seed, and $R_{50}$ and $R_{30}$, the inverse background efficiencies at 50\% and 30\% signal efficiency. On JetClass-II and JetClass the AUC is the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve, averaged over the classes, each against the rest. With JetClass's equal class sizes this equals the average over class pairs that the Particle Transformer paper reports. On JetClass-II the average is also given over the classes with the full \JcBalTestPerClass{} test jets (full count), over the resonant classes and over the QCD classes. The linear probes on top and quark/gluon tagging set the missing inputs to \LpSubstitutionTop{} for top and to \LpSubstitutionQg{} for quark/gluon, as chosen on validation. The network pretrained with \Kfull{} classes on kinematic inputs only has one run, on top tagging only, and the released Sophon checkpoint, set apart by a rule, is outside the controlled comparison. The batch-normalization (BatchNorm) statistics of the random network with BatchNorm recomputed are re-estimated on \DesignBnJets{} training jets. A flavor boundary keeps apart decays that differ only in quark flavor. Values are the mean $\pm$ standard deviation over \MlstNRunsWord{} runs, \MlstMassNRunsWord{} per mass network and flavor-boundary partition. Each random partition is a single run on its own row. $^{\S}$~marks a value from a single run. $^{\P}$~marks a value whose runs agree to the digits shown.}\label{tab:probesfull}\\
