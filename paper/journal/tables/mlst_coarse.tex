\captionof{table}{Linear probes on the frozen features of the networks pretrained with 188, 162, 43 and 17 classes, of the two networks with an added mass output, of the 17-class partitions and of the random network, compared on coarser versions of the JetClass-II task. The probes are trained on one million class-balanced JetClass-II jets whose 188 classes are merged into the 43 or the 17 classes of the pretraining label sets or into the 17 classes of the partition with no flavor boundary. A flavor boundary keeps apart decays that differ only in quark flavor. AUC is the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve, averaged over the target's groups, each against the rest. Values are the mean $\pm$ standard deviation over \MlstNRunsWord{} runs, \MlstMassNRunsWord{} per mass network and flavor-boundary partition, and over five random partitions. Bold marks values within three standard deviations (both added in quadrature, before rounding) of the best in their column. A value without a standard deviation counts with none. The random network is a reference, never bold. $^{\P}$~marks a value whose runs agree to the digits shown.}
