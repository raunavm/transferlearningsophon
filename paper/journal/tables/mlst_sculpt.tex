\captionof{table}{Mass sculpting of QCD jets by the output scores of the networks pretrained with 188, 162, 43 and 17 classes and of the control networks. The distance is the Jensen--Shannon distance (base 2) $\times10^{2}$ between the soft-drop mass distributions of all QCD test jets and of those passing a cut on $1-g_{\mathrm{QCD}}$, one minus the summed output of the QCD classes. The cut keeps the fraction $\epsB$ of the QCD jets or half of the resonant test jets, the \LitJetClassTwoNResonantClasses{} resonant classes taken together ($\epsS = 50\%$), where $R_{50} = 1/\epsB$, and $\epsS$ beside a distance is the fraction of the resonant test jets that pass. The last row is the distance expected for a random selection of as many QCD test jets. A flavor boundary is a class boundary between decays that differ only in quark flavor. Values are the mean $\pm$ standard deviation over \MlstNRunsWord{} pretraining runs per label set, \RandFlavRuns{} per flavor-boundary partition and \MlstMassNRunsWord{} per network with an added mass output.
