\captionof{table}{Linear probes on the frozen features and fine-tuning of the networks pretrained with 188, 162, 43 and 17 classes and with the 17-class partitions, compared on class-balanced JetClass-II, JetClass, top tagging and quark/gluon tagging. A flavor boundary is a class boundary between decays that differ only in quark flavor. $R_{30}$ is the inverse background efficiency at 30\% signal efficiency. On JetClass-II and JetClass, AUC is the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve, averaged over the classes, each against the rest. Linear probes use each task's largest training set, and fine-tuning uses one million JetClass-II jets, one million JetClass jets and the full top and quark/gluon training sets. Values are the mean $\pm$ standard deviation over \MlstNRunsWord{} pretraining runs per label set, \RandFlavRuns{} per flavor-boundary partition, one for each of five random partitions, three initializations of the random network and three training seeds from scratch. Bold marks the best value of each block of a column and every value within three standard deviations of it. $^{\P}$ marks a value whose standard deviation over the runs rounds to zero at the digits shown.}
