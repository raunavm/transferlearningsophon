On JetClass-II and JetClass, AUC is the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve, averaged over the classes, each against the rest. $R_{50}$ and $R_{30}$ are the inverse background efficiencies at 50\% and 30\% signal efficiency. The network with kinematic inputs is pretrained on those inputs only and lacks none, and the released Sophon checkpoint has the missing inputs set to zero. A flavor boundary is a class boundary between decays that differ only in quark flavor.
