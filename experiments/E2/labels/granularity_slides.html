How fine is fine enough?01 / 09

The question

Does a finer pre-training label help the representation?

Same architecture, same jets, same compute — only the number of training labels changes. We pre-train the backbone at several granularities and ask whether 188 fine labels teach a better representation than 2, 10, or 30 coarse ones.

Too coarse

2 classes (signal vs QCD) — the model may never learn distinctions it's never asked about.

Too fine

188 classes — many are near-degenerate; the label may be noise the backbone wastes capacity on.

The sweet spot?

Somewhere in between — this deck is about defining the rungs so we can measure it.

The control

A random 10-class split (physics ignored). If semantic-10 beats it, the grouping matters — not just the count.

The proposal

A refinement ladder: 2 ⊇ 10 ⊇ 30 ⊇ 188

Each coarser rung is an exact union of finer classes. Two rungs are given: 30 is Sophon's own reweighting grouping (primary source); 10 is our proposal — the review target.

Decisions for Prof. Duarte

Open grouping questions

Primary cut. Is single-X vs X→YY the right primary split, or should prong count (2/3/4) be primary regardless of one-vs-two resonances?

Leptonic single-X. Keep ℓℓ and ττ together (group 2), or split — they behave very differently in a jet.

τ handling. We separate hadronic-τ from light-lepton groups. Right seam, or collapse all τ together?

Gluon content. Group 3 mixes QQQQ with gggg. Should all-gluon final states be their own class?

QCD. Kept as one class. Split by heavy flavor (native 27, or coarse {light, c, b}) so the model learns flavor in background too?

The number. Is 10 the right coarse rung, or would 8 / 12 be more physically natural? The ladder can carry any count.

Caveats & next steps

Before we freeze

  • The JetClass-I analogue is an analogy, not a mapping. JetClass-II is mass-scanned — no direct Z→qq / W→qq / leptonic-top classes. Only ~7 of the 10 JC-I categories have a structural counterpart here.
  • The grouping is revisable until the config freeze (Gate G0). Any change goes through make_label_maps.py so the JSON, the appendix, and the config YAMLs stay in lockstep.
  • The random-10 control is frozen alongside — same group sizes, physics ignored — so semantic-vs-count is a clean comparison.
  • Reference material: granularity_grouping_for_review.md (full write-up) · label_maps_appendix.md (every group's members) · label_maps.json (machine-readable).
